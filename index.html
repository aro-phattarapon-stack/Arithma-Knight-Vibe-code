TACTICAL FIELD MANUAL

1. Turn Flow & Action Points (3 AP)

Each round, you receive 3 Action Points and a fresh shared Number Pool (4 stones).

  • Equip Numbers (Free Action / 0 AP): Select a number stone from the pool, then choose a living hero to arm them for the round.
  • Execute Skills (Costs 1 AP): Select an armed hero to ready their stance, then target an anomaly or ally to unleash their arithmetic strike.
  • End Turn: Round concludes when AP reaches 0, or press END TURN at any moment to preserve party advantage.

2. The Four Arithmetic Orders

Knight (−) • 40 Vitality

The Subtractor. Cleaves enemy vitality by subtracting his held number directly. Precise, reliable, and lethal against wounded foes.

Berserker (÷) • 35 Vitality

The Divider. Delivers catastrophic dividing cleaves that halve and quarter enemy health pools. Unrivaled against titanic bosses.

Cleric (+) • 30 Vitality

The Adder. Channels sacred restoration, adding her held number to mend ally wounds and preserve the party gauntlet.

Paladin (×) • 45 Vitality

The Multiplier. Charges holy fervor (+1x each round, up to ×3), empowering an armed ally's stone by multiplying its potency.
